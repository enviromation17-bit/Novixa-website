Days 1–5: Fix your foundation

Every day:

Writing

10–15 sentence-production drills
1 body paragraph
Task 1 or Task 2 every other day

Speaking

20–30 minutes of live questions with me
I'll interrupt only when necessary
We'll target tense switching, sentence assembly, and natural connectors

Reading

1 full passage
Analyze why each wrong answer was wrong

Listening

1 section at first, then progressively more
Track spelling, plurals, numbers, and distractors
Days 6–10: Exam pressure

We'll move toward:

full Reading tests
full Listening tests
timed Task 1
timed Task 2
Speaking Part 1 → Part 2 → Part 3 simulations

And I'll keep an error log for you.

Days 11–14: Full simulations

At least 3 complete IELTS simulations.

No help during the test.

Then we'll analyze:

What mistake happened?
Why did it happen?
Is it knowledge, vocabulary, attention, or automaticity?
How do we stop it?

Day 15: Light review

No exhausting full tests.

We'll review your recurring errors, useful structures, speaking topics, and test strategy.
